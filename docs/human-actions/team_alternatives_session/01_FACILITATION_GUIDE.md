## Agenda

### Block 1 — Free brainstorm (15 min) → closes A5

- Rule: **every** idea is recorded, including bad, duplicate, and immediately discarded ones. Discarded ideas are marked "discarded + reason", never deleted.
- Prompt: "Independent of the research package — what are all the ways these two jurisdictions could get public EV charging?"
- Scribe records all ideas verbatim in the session record (07, section 1).
- Do not open the provisional MCDA before this block ends (avoid anchoring).

### Block 2 — Confirm/amend the alternatives table (15 min) → closes A6

- Open the seven researched alternatives (A–G). Team asks: Are these genuinely distinct? Is anything from Block 1 missing? Should any be merged, split, renamed, or added?
- Each amendment (or confirmation of no amendment) is recorded with a reason in 07, section 2.

### Block 3 — MCDA walk (30 min) → closes A7, B7

- Facilitator presents `02_PROVISIONAL_MCDA.md` explicitly as **PROVISIONAL — researched scores the team must review, amend, and adopt**.
- First: adopt or amend the **weights** using `04_WEIGHT_ADOPTION_PAGE.md` — including reading aloud the equity-weighting crossover disclosure before deciding.
- Then walk the matrix row by row. **Any member may re-score any cell** by stating a reason; the scribe records the change on `03_TEAM_SCORING_SHEET.md` with the member's name and reason.
- **Dissent is recorded, not resolved away**: if a member disagrees with an adopted score or weight, it goes in `05_DISSENT_LOG.md` verbatim. Consensus is not required; documentation is.
- Recompute the weighted totals with any changed scores/weights before Block 4 (arithmetic in 03).

### Block 4 — Adopt the winner + conditions (10 min) → closes A8, feeds G5, F2

- Team adopts a leading solution (which may or may not be the researched leader B) **with conditions**: what client input, data, or event would change it. Record in `07_DECISION_RECORD_TEMPLATE.md` — signed/initialed by attendees.
- Explicitly log what client input from the CM2 follow-up could change the adoption (feeds G5).
- Confirm the funding/budget framing the team will present (F2 linkage: FY2027 adopted budgets, Dominion make-ready, NEVI-with-caveat).
