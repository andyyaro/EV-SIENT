## MANDATORY DISCLOSURE — equity-weighting crossover

> The default weights place equity at 10/100. Raising it far enough makes the **Hybrid
> portfolio (F) overtake Staged pilots (B)**, because F carries broader geographic value via
> EV-ready seeds at lower-tier sites. In other words: **the choice of equity weight can
> decide the outcome.** The team must choose the weight deliberately, knowing this, and may
> revisit it after the client answers priority question Q3 (equity of access vs
> utilization-proven expansion).
>
> **Where the crossover actually is: roughly 2.5× the default (~25/100), not 2×.**
> Recomputed directly from the matrix in `02_PROVISIONAL_MCDA.md`, reducing the other five
> weights proportionally: at equity **20** B still leads **82.2 to 80.9**; at equity **25**
> they **tie at 80.8**; above 25 F leads. B and F differ on only three criteria — F is better
> on equity by one point and worse on both cost and data confidence by one point each — so at
> the default weights B leads by exactly 4.0 points, and only equity weight closes that gap.

**Correction note (2026-07-27).** This page previously stated the crossover at 2×, citing
Report 04 §2, which is a research-phase document not tracked in this repository. The 2.5×
figure above is recomputed from the published matrix and is what the team should rely on when
adopting weights. The discrepancy has not been reconciled against Report 04 itself, because
that document is not available here — if it is recovered, check which input differs rather
than assuming this correction supersedes it. Understating the threshold makes the outcome look
more fragile to equity weighting than the matrix supports, which could push the vote either way.

*(A further caveat, for completeness: F can be made to beat B at equity 20, but only by
setting the cost and data-confidence weights to zero — the two criteria on which F is worse.

was describing.)*
