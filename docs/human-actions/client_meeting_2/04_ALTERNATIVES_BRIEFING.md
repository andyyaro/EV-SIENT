# Alternatives Briefing - Client Version

*Format per toolkit §6: name → one-sentence concept → what you'd see in year 1 → 10-yr cost order → main risk → what evidence would change our mind. No jargon. The county's fleet hub is presented as "your parallel track we coordinate with," never as a competing recommendation. All seven researched alternatives are shown here for completeness; the client slide condenses to four rows.*

## A. Broad immediate distributed buildout

- **Concept:** Install substantial active charging at many sites now.
- **Year 1:** Dozens of new ports across both jurisdictions, most idle most of the day.
- **10-yr cost order:** Highest of the public options.
- **Main risk:** Repeating Neabsco - paying to install, then paying (~$1,000/station/yr scale) to keep or decommission underused stations.
- **What would change our mind:** Verified electrical capacity at many sites plus evidence of much faster near-term EV adoption than our scenarios show.

## B. Staged distributed pilots with expandable pathways - *our current leader*

- **Concept:** Small 2–3-port pilots at the top-ranked sites, with conduit and panel space oversized during the initial dig so expansion is cheap when utilization triggers fire.
- **Year 1:** 2–3 working ports at each of up to three sites; visible service; expansion capacity in the ground but not energized.
- **10-yr cost order:** Low-moderate; avoids both the retrofit-trenching and the overbuild failure modes you described.
- **Main risk:** Slower geographic reach - equity benefits wait for expansion triggers.
- **What would change our mind:** Client priority on immediate broad coverage, or utilization far above our scenarios.

## C. EV-ready first (pathways only, minimal active chargers)

- **Concept:** Build the conduit/panel pathways now (your Animal Services Center pattern) and energize later.
- **Year 1:** Almost nothing visible - capped conduit and reserved panel space; little or no service.
- **10-yr cost order:** Lowest initial capital of the build options.
- **Main risk:** Delivers no near-term charging or visible public value; the Animal Services conduit still has no charger.
- **What would change our mind:** A budget collapse - this is the floor option.

## D. Centralized fleet hub plus selective public charging - *your parallel track we coordinate with*

- **Concept:** Concentrate charging at a government complex, as the county is already pursuing with its ~35-station fleet hub now in design.
- **Year 1:** County hub design progresses on its own track; our public-site plan coordinates siting and load with it rather than duplicating it.
- **10-yr cost order:** Moderate-high for the county; not the team's public-site answer.
- **Main risk:** ADA space-clustering redesign cost (which you and Dominion both reported) and absent fleet duty-cycle data.
- **What would change our mind:** If you tell us fleet electrification is the 24-month priority, this becomes the frame and our model becomes a siting layer inside it.

## E. Managed-charging-first

- **Concept:** Prioritize load management/smart charging so electrical upgrades are deferred.
- **Year 1:** Software-managed charging at whatever ports exist; deferred service upgrades.
- **10-yr cost order:** Low-moderate; savings come from avoided upgrades.
- **Main risk:** Depends on networked chargers and reliable communications with fail-safe design; it is not a siting strategy by itself.
- **What would change our mind:** Nothing to abandon - this layers onto B/F as ports multiply.

## F. Hybrid portfolio - *close second*

- **Concept:** Staged pilots (B) as the spine, EV-ready seeding (C) at lower-tier sites, managed charging (E) as the operating layer, coordinated with your fleet-hub track (D).
- **Year 1:** Same visible pilots as B, plus cheap EV-ready seeds at a few lower-ranked sites.
- **10-yr cost order:** Slightly above B.
- **Main risk:** More moving parts to explain and govern.
- **What would change our mind:** If geographic equity outweighs utilization-proven expansion in your priorities, this overtakes B in our comparison.

## G. Do nothing / defer (honest comparator)

- **Concept:** Baseline for honesty - defer all deployment.
- **Year 1:** Nothing.
- **10-yr cost order:** Zero direct cost; wasted proactive-planning window (our fire-service interviewee's point).
- **Main risk:** Zero service and equity value; the planning window closes.
- **What would change our mind:** Nothing plausible; it exists so the comparison is honest.
