## 1. Problem recap (one paragraph)

Fairfax City and Prince William County need public Level 2 EV charging that grows with demand without repeating two documented local failure modes: retrofit cost escalation when expansion pathways are not built during initial civil work (your McCoart 10-port trenching experience), and overbuilt capacity that sits underused and costs money to keep or shut off (Neabsco garage: 70 ports, 17 stations later taken offline at roughly $1,000/station/yr). Our Phase 2 work screens 12 public candidate sites across both jurisdictions, models demand and capacity at 10/20/30-year horizons, and compares seven genuinely distinct deployment strategies.
