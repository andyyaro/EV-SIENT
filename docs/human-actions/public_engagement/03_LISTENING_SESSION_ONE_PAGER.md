## Public EV Charging at Your Library and Government Center — We're Listening

**Who we are:** A Future City Fellowship student team working with the Prince William County Office of Sustainability and the City of Fairfax.

### What's decided

**Nothing.** No chargers have been approved, funded, or sited. No fees have been set. This session happens *before* decisions, which is the point.

### What's proposed (for your reaction)

- **Small pilots, not big buildouts:** 2–3 Level 2 charging ports at a few high-scoring public sites (candidates: City of Fairfax Regional Library, Fairfax County Government Center, Bull Run Library).
- **Expandable by design:** extra conduit and electrical room installed during the first dig, so adding ports later is cheap — but ports are only added when real usage triggers say so. This county has already experienced both failure modes we are avoiding: chargers nobody used (and paid to shut off), and expansions that cost multiples because pathways weren't pre-built.
- **Everyone can pay:** tap-to-pay without a smartphone app is a design requirement.
- **Accessible-first layouts**, reviewed with accessible users before construction.

### What your feedback changes

- **Siting:** which sites go first, and site-level "no" conditions.
- **Layout:** where in the lot, which spaces convert, how many ADA-adjacent.
- **Fees:** no fee policy exists; your input goes to the officials who will set one.
- **Expansion priorities:** how we balance proven-usage expansion against fair geographic coverage — including for areas where EV adoption is currently lower.
