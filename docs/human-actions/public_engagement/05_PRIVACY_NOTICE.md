## Your privacy in this study

**No personal information is required to comment.** You can complete the survey, submit a comment card, or speak at a session without giving your name, address, email, or phone number. The only location question (home ZIP code) is optional and used solely to understand who each site serves.

**The website assistant explains — it does not collect.** Our project website's assistant exists to explain the study. It is not a channel for formal public comment, and you should not enter personal information into it. **Formal comments belong in accountable municipal channels** ([FORMAL CHANNEL(S) TO BE CONFIRMED with Fairfax City and Prince William County]), where public-record rules and accountability apply.

**What we keep and for how long (retention statement):** Anonymous survey responses and session notes are kept for the duration of the fellowship project and any resulting municipal planning use, then handled per the applicable municipal records-retention schedule. If you voluntarily give contact information (for example, to receive a summary of what changed), it is used only for that purpose, never sold or shared for marketing, and deleted when that purpose is complete. Comments made through official municipal channels are governed by those bodies' public-records rules, which may make them public records.

**Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA) awareness:** This study is designed to minimize data collection so that, for most participants, no personal data is processed at all. Where personal data is voluntarily provided, we honor requests to access, correct, or delete it — contact [PROJECT CONTACT CHANNEL TO BE CONFIRMED].

**Photos:** No photographs of identifiable individuals are taken at site walks or sessions without explicit consent.
