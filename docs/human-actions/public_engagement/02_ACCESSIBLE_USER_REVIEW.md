# Accessible-User Review - Invitation and Protocol

*Context: ADA EV-space requirements are the documented local cost/schedule driver (client's fleet-hub redesign; Dominion reports the same). The US Access Board EVSE rule is still at NPRM stage (no final rule as of 2026-07-19), so review by actual accessible users against Access Board technical-assistance guidance is our strongest available design check. Recipient organizations to be identified by the team (disability advocacy organizations serving Fairfax City / Prince William County) - placeholders only; no invitation has been sent.*

## Invitation text (draft)

**To:** [DISABILITY ADVOCACY ORGANIZATION / ACCESSIBILITY COORDINATOR CONTACT]
**Subject:** Invitation - accessibility review of proposed public EV-charging layouts (Fairfax City / Prince William County)

Dear [NAME],

Our Future City Fellowship team, working with the PWC Office of Sustainability and the City of Fairfax, is designing small public EV-charging pilots at three sites: City of Fairfax Regional Library, Fairfax County Government Center, and Bull Run Library. Local experience shows accessibility requirements are where EV-charging projects here have actually incurred redesign - so we want accessible users reviewing layouts **before** anything is built, not after.

We invite you (and members you may nominate) to:

1. **Review layout drawings** - accessible route, space and aisle dimensions, charger reach ranges, cable management, bollard placement, signage - on paper or by video call (30–45 min); and/or
2. **Join a site walk** at one or more of the three sites to test the proposed locations in person (60 min, scheduled at your convenience; accessible meeting point at the main entrance).

Your comments will be recorded verbatim in our feedback register, mapped to specific design changes, and reported back to you with what changed and why. Participation involves no obligation and no personal information beyond what you choose to share.

[TEAM SENDER + CONTACT CHANNEL]

## Protocol (for the team)

1. **Materials:** dimensioned layout drawing per site; Access Board TA-guidance checklist; camera for (consented) photos of pinch points.
2. **Site-walk script:** approach from parking and from transit stop; simulate charge connection at charger height; test door/ramp clearances; review signage sightlines at day and dusk.
3. **Capture:** one scribe records verbatim comments; each comment gets an ID and enters `06_FEEDBACK_TO_DECISION_MATRIX.md`.
4. **Close the loop:** written summary of resulting design changes sent to participants; disagreements recorded, not smoothed over.
5. **No PII beyond voluntary contact for the loop-closing summary;** see 05_PRIVACY_NOTICE.md.
