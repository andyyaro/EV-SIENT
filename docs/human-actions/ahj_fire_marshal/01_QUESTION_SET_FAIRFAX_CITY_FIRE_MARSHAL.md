## A. Code basis

1. Do you review EV charging installations under the 2021 Virginia SFPC as currently adopted (2021 USBC effective 2024-01-18, 2020 NEC electrical basis)? Are there any local amendments or standing interpretations affecting EVSE?
2. The 2024 Virginia code-development cycle is underway (expected conclusion ≈2027). Should we design to the adopted 2021 editions only, or do you advise anticipating any specific change?

## B. Disconnect location, labeling, illumination

3. For public Level 2 EVSE (≤19.2 kW AC pedestal units), where do you want the emergency/service disconnect located relative to the equipment — and is a single remote disconnect for a bank of ports acceptable?
4. What labeling do you require or prefer at the disconnect and at the equipment (wording, size, reflectivity, photoluminescence)?
5. Do you require the disconnect and its signage to be illuminated or visible at night, and to what acceptance test?

## C. Apparatus access geometry

6. What clearances do you require between EVSE/pedestals/bollards and fire lanes, hydrants, FDCs, and building access paths at surface lots like our library sites?
7. Is there a minimum aisle/turning geometry you apply when charging rows change parking layout?

## D. Stormwater / firewater runoff

8. In an EVSE-fire suppression event, do you have expectations for containment or drainage of firewater runoff at surface lots (coordination point with City Public Works as the VSMP authority)?
