# Proposed-Standard Feedback Sheet - AHJ Reaction Request

*One-page companion to the question sets. Our fellowship team is proposing a new municipal EV-charging standard concept (see `docs/standards/PROPOSED_MUNICIPAL_EV_STANDARD_CONCEPT.md`). We are asking the Fire Marshal's office to react to it as practitioners - especially Module M1. This is a student fellowship concept, not a code proposal before any body.*

## The concept in brief

Scope: public municipal charging sites (new and retrofit), L1/L2 AC (DC annex reserved). Test modules include:

- **M1 - Emergency access & disconnect (flagship, most testable):** apparatus approach dimensions; disconnect location/labeling/illumination/operation time; responder signage.
- **M2 - Expandability & right-sizing documentation:** pathway capacity declaration; utilization-monitoring capability; documented expansion triggers; load-management fail-safe behavior on communications loss.
- **M3 - Accessibility & site conditions cross-references:** accessible route/space conformance by reference to Access Board guidance; impact protection; enclosure/garage conditions; stormwater/firewater planning note.

## Questions for the AHJ (M1 focus)

1. Are the M1 elements (approach dimensions, disconnect location/labeling/illumination, operation-time test, responder signage) the right list? What is missing or unnecessary from a responder's standpoint?
2. What **measurable pass/fail values** would you set for: maximum distance from apparatus position to disconnect; disconnect operation time; signage legibility distance at night?
3. Would an M1-style commissioning test (witnessed disconnect operation + signage check before energization) be practical within your current inspection workflow?
4. Does M2's "fail-safe on comms loss" behavior matter to your office (equipment defaulting to a safe charging state), or is that purely an electrical-inspection matter?
5. For M3: for enclosed garages (Neabsco-class), what conditions would you want the standard to reference?
6. Overall: would a standard like this make your review of municipal charging sites easier, harder, or no different - and why?
