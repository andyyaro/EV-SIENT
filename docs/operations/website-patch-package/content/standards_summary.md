## Standards that govern our design intent

**Highlighted ULSE standards**
- **UL 2594** (Electric Vehicle Supply Equipment, 3rd ed. 2022) — safety standard for the
  exact equipment class proposed (AC Level 2). Procurement will require UL 2594-listed
  equipment; commissioning verifies listings and personnel protection.
- **UL 3600** (Measuring and Reporting Circular Economy Aspects, 2023) — sustainability
  standard anchoring vendor circularity disclosure, annual circularity reporting, and
  end-of-life/take-back planning.

**Essential supporting requirements:** UL 2231-1/-2 personnel protection; NEC Article 625
as adopted in Virginia (2020 NEC via the 2021 USBC, effective 2024-01-18); Virginia
USBC/SFPC; US Access Board EVSE accessibility guidance (rule at NPRM stage as of July
2026; VA USBC ADA provisions govern today); OCPP and ISO 15118 as supporting
interoperability protocols (not ULSE standards). UL 2202 (DC charging) and UL 9540/9540A +
NFPA 855 (storage) apply **only if** DC fast charging or battery storage enter scope —
neither is in the current plan.

**Proposed standard (fellowship design concept — not an official UL/ULSE proposal):**
*Municipal EV Charging Site Readiness and Emergency Access Standard* — a site-level
readiness framework with test modules for emergency access/disconnect, expandability
documentation, accessibility cross-references, monitoring/triggers, managed-charging
fail-safe, commissioning evidence, and end-of-life. Unsourced parameters are marked TBV
for ULSE/AHJ/manufacturer validation.
