## Results at 10 / 20 / 30 years (2036 · 2046 · 2056)

All values from canonical contract v3.0.0 (generated 2026-10-18, DEC-027); screening
estimates with labeled assumptions - not forecasts, budgets, or approvals. The official
pathway is 47 ports. An earlier version of this page, built from contract v1.0.0
(2026-07-19), showed the `legacy_v2` plan's 48-port pathway; that is history, not a second
current figure.

### Aspect 1 - ports and service capacity (base → high adoption paths)
| Horizon | Active ports (base) | Active ports (high) | Pathway ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 (baseline) | 25 | 25 | 47 |
| 2036 | 31 | 43 | 47 |
| 2046 | 43 | 47 | 47 |
| 2056 | 43 | 47 | 47 |

Activation is trigger-gated: ports energize only when observed utilization, wait, and
reliability evidence (with an equity override) clears a proposed policy that itself
requires human approval.

### Aspect 2 - lifecycle cost (portfolio NPV, screening, base escalation)

|---|---|---|---|
| 2036 | $1.49M | $1.85M | **$0.36M** |
| 2046 | $2.21M | $2.60M | **$0.38M** |

### Environmental outcome (attached to Aspect 2)
Net avoided CO2e grows from ≈43 t/yr (2026 baseline) to ≈173 t/yr (2056 base path;
≈260 t/yr high path), computed with the EPA eGRID2023 Rev 2 SRVC factor
(596.3 lb CO2e/MWh, refreshed annually) and labeled displacement assumptions. The staged
plan also avoids energizing pathway ports that current demand does not justify: **22 at the
2026 baseline, 16 by 2036, and 4 by 2046 and 2056** on the base path, as demand catches up
with the pre-built capacity. That narrowing is the point - staging defers spend it cannot yet
justify, and stops deferring once the evidence arrives. It is the resource-management lesson
of the county's own decommissioning experience.
