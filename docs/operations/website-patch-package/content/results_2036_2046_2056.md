## Results at 10 / 20 / 30 years (2036 · 2046 · 2056)

All values from canonical contract v1.0.0 (generated 2026-07-19); screening estimates
with labeled assumptions - not forecasts, budgets, or approvals.

Activation is trigger-gated: ports energize only when observed utilization, wait, and
reliability evidence (with an equity override) clears a proposed policy that itself
requires human approval.

### Environmental outcome (attached to Aspect 2)
Net avoided CO2e grows from ≈43 t/yr (2026 baseline) to ≈173 t/yr (2056 base path;
≈260 t/yr high path), computed with the EPA eGRID2023 Rev 2 SRVC factor
(596.3 lb CO2e/MWh, refreshed annually) and labeled displacement assumptions. The staged
plan also avoids energizing pathway ports that current demand does not justify: **23 at the
2026 baseline, 13 by 2036, and 4 by 2046 and 2056** on the base path, as demand catches up
with the pre-built capacity. That narrowing is the point - staging defers spend it cannot yet
justify, and stops deferring once the evidence arrives. It is the resource-management lesson
of the county's own decommissioning experience.
