### Environmental outcome (attached to Aspect 2)
Net avoided CO2e grows from ≈43 t/yr (2026 baseline) to ≈173 t/yr (2056 base path;
≈260 t/yr high path), computed with the EPA eGRID2023 Rev 2 SRVC factor
(596.3 lb CO2e/MWh, refreshed annually) and labeled displacement assumptions. The staged
plan also avoids energizing 13–23 pathway ports that current demand does not justify —
the resource-management lesson of the county's own decommissioning experience.
