# Privacy Notice (July 2026)

- **Reading this site requires no personal information.** We do not require registration,
  and we collect no personal data beyond standard, short-lived technical logs.
- **The AI Assistant is restricted to approved, authenticated users** (Cognito). It is an
  *explanation tool* for the project's methods and documents. Assistant conversations may
  be logged for quality and security; they are not used for any other purpose and are not
  shared.
- **Formal public comments do not belong here.** To make your voice count in an official
  process, use the accountable municipal channels listed on our engagement page; we will
  never treat website or assistant activity as formal public input.
- **Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA) posture:** we practice data minimization,
  purpose limitation, and time-limited retention of technical logs. Questions or requests:
  use the contact route on the engagement page.
- We do not sell or share any visitor data. No advertising or cross-site tracking is used.
