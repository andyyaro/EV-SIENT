EV charging site readiness

Twelve candidate sites in Northern Virginia, screened for whether public charging could make sense — and if so, how much to start with.

Nothing here is approved, funded or permitted. Electrical capacity is a placeholder at all twelve sites — nobody has confirmed the service could support chargers. Demand is modelled, not measured. Costs are screening estimates for comparison, never budgets or quotes.
