| Stormwater | VSMP-delegated local programs | City Public Works | PWC Environmental Services |
| Utility interconnection / tariffs | Dominion (SCC-regulated) (EX12) | Dominion — same for both jurisdictions | Dominion |
| Procurement | VPPA (Virginia Public Procurement Act) processes | City purchasing | PWC Procurement Services |
| Data / privacy (website & assistant) | VCDPA (Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act) considerations; municipal records rules | City counsel / IT | County counsel / DoIT |

Every lane above already appears in the team's gate model; this table adds the missing piece the audit flagged (G1): **who decides.** Final interpretation of adopted editions rests with each AHJ.

## 3. Engagement posture (from Report 10, for reference — nothing sent this phase)

High-fit approaches: facility-user intercept/online surveys at top-3 sites; accessibility-community review of layouts; listening sessions riding existing civic meetings. Web/assistant channels are for **explanation, not collection** (VCDPA-aware, no unnecessary PII); formal comments stay in accountable human channels. Language access: Spanish at minimum for PWC candidates. Every engagement input maps to a design response via the decision-log template in the engagement toolkit.

## 4. Social-implication flags carried into design

Equity override in expansion triggers (so low-adoption areas are not permanently starved); parking displacement counted and stated (libraries are parking-sensitive); fee policy flagged as a client decision affecting equity and any future ROI; EVITP-certified installer requirement adopted as standard practice; battery-fire perception answered factually (zero local EV fires per Adams interview); no-app/tap-to-pay capability required in procurement for digital access.
