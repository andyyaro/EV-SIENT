Standards

The codes and standards behind the project’s design intent, and when each one applies.

How Standards Are Used Here

Standards set the design intent for this planning-support prototype: what procurement would require, what commissioning would verify, and what would be reported over the equipment’s life.

Naming a standard here is not a code-compliance determination. Every siting decision remains subject to electrical, ADA, fire/AHJ, stormwater and permitting review by the responsible authorities, including the authority having jurisdiction (AHJ).

  • PrimaryGoverns the equipment and practices the plan proposes.
  • SupportingEssential requirements and protocols that work alongside the primary pair.
  • ConditionalApplies only if DC fast charging or battery storage enter scope. Neither is in the current plan.
  • ProposedA fellowship design concept, not an adopted or official standard.

Primary Standards

The pair that governs the plan’s design intent.

  • UL 2594PrimaryElectric Vehicle Supply Equipment, 3rd edition (2022)The safety standard for the exact equipment class proposed: AC Level 2.Procurement will require UL 2594-listed equipment; commissioning verifies listings and personnel protection.
  • UL 3600PrimaryMeasuring and Reporting Circular Economy Aspects (2023)The sustainability standard that anchors vendor circularity disclosure, annual circularity reporting, and end-of-life and take-back planning.
The two highlighted ULSE standards.

Supporting Requirements

Essential requirements that sit alongside the primary pair.

  • UL 2231-1 and UL 2231-2SupportingPersonnel protectionPersonnel protection requirements for EV charging circuits.
  • NEC Article 625SupportingNational Electrical CodeAs adopted in Virginia: the 2020 NEC, through the 2021 Virginia Uniform Statewide Building Code (USBC), effective 2024-01-18.
  • Virginia USBC and SFPCSupportingState building and fire codesThe Virginia Uniform Statewide Building Code and Statewide Fire Prevention Code.
  • US Access Board EVSE accessibility guidanceSupportingAccessibilityThe rule was at the proposed-rule (NPRM) stage as of July 2026. The ADA provisions of the Virginia USBC govern today.
  • OCPPSupportingInteroperability protocolOpen Charge Point Protocol. A supporting interoperability protocol, not a ULSE standard.
  • ISO 15118SupportingInteroperability protocolA supporting interoperability protocol for vehicle-to-charger communication, not a ULSE standard.

Conditional Standards
