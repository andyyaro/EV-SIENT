Where public EV charging can start

A screening of 12 public sites (libraries, government centers, a commuter garage and a training center) for starter EV charging now, with room to grow later.

What this site is, and is not

A planning-support prototype. It is not engineering advice, a permit, or a utility interconnection decision, and no site shown here is approved. Electrical capacity is an unverified placeholder at all twelve sites, so no ranking here is a feasibility finding.
