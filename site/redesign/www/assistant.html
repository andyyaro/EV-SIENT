What You Can Ask

Questions about how the study works and what its results mean.

  • How is the readiness score calculated?
  • Why does FFX004 rank first in the canonical model?
  • What is the difference between starter and pathway ports?
  • Why is data confidence Low at every site?
  • What does avoided CO2e measure, and why is higher better?
  • Which standards does the plan rely on, and which are conditional?
  • What would need to be verified before a site could advance?
Examples only. The assistant covers the project purpose, model logic, methodology, datasets, assumptions, results, limitations, the codebase, and how to read the public results. Keep each message focused and concise for the best answers.

Limits

  • Scores are prototype planning indicators, not final site rankings or official recommendations.
  • Demand estimates depend on available data, assumptions, and simplified future-growth scenarios.
  • Electrical feasibility uses proxy or manual planning inputs where utility-grade capacity details are unavailable.
  • ADA layout, fire access, stormwater, and construction constraints still require professional field review.
  • Results should be used to compare and prioritize candidate sites, not to approve installation.
  • Answers are generated by an AI model and can be incomplete or wrong. Check anything important against the Method and Standards pages.
Planning support only. The model is a screening and exploration tool. It does not provide official engineering, permitting, utility, procurement, fire, ADA, stormwater, construction, or site-approval decisions.

Privacy

  • Sign-in requiredThe assistant is restricted to approved, authenticated users. Accounts are managed with Amazon Cognito on the current project site, and access is by invitation.
  • Conversations may be loggedLogs are kept for quality and security. They are not used for any other purpose and are not shared.
  • Not for personal dataDo not enter passwords, credentials, API keys, personally sensitive information, or confidential project details.
  • Not for formal commentsFormal public comments belong in the accountable municipal channels. Assistant activity is never treated as formal public input.
