What this site is — and is not. This is a Future City Fellowship planning-support prototype. It is not engineering advice, a permit or code-compliance determination, a utility capacity confirmation, an official forecast, or a procurement or construction authorization. Every siting decision remains subject to electrical, ADA, fire/AHJ, stormwater and permitting review by the responsible authorities. No site shown here is approved. Electrical capacity is an unverified placeholder at all twelve sites — nobody has confirmed the service could support chargers.
