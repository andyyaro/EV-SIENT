Planning-support only

This model is a screening and exploration tool. It does not provide official engineering, permitting, utility, procurement, fire, ADA, stormwater, construction, or site-approval decisions.

Model limitations

  • Scores are prototype planning indicators, not final site rankings or official recommendations.
  • Demand estimates depend on available data, assumptions, and simplified future-growth scenarios.
  • Electrical feasibility uses proxy/manual planning inputs where utility-grade capacity details are unavailable.
  • ADA layout, fire access, stormwater, and construction constraints still require professional field review.
  • Results should be used to compare and prioritize candidate sites, not to approve installation.

Privacy and safe use

Do not enter private credentials, API keys, passwords, personally sensitive information, or confidential project details. For best results, keep each message focused and concise.
