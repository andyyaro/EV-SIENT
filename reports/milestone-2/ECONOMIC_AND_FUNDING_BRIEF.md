## 3. Recommended pilot funding path

1. **Dominion Level 2 Charging Program first** - apply early; it fits the exact equipment class, includes interconnection review (retiring risk R01/R02 work), and the disadvantaged-community 100% option should be screened against PWC candidates (IM-07).
2. Local CIP/budget entry as the appropriation vehicle (both FY2027 CIPs are adopted; a pilot rides the next cycle or an existing facilities program).

4. Budget-shortfall floor: pilot-only deployment - Alternative C degrades gracefully, which is a selling point, not a concession (Report 09 §5).

## 4. Cost structure and lifecycle screening frame

- **Benchmarks (EX15):** average L2 install ≈ $2,500/connector (range $1k–$10k driven by trenching/panel/labor); maintenance ≈ $400/yr per L2 plus network/software fees; NREL: soft costs can exceed 50% of project cost. Client frame: ~$1,000/station/yr keep-online; 17 decommissioned stations ≈ $20k/yr avoided.
- **Client cost evidence:** trenching/undergrounding distance dominated McCoart cost growth - hence the model's trenching-minimization criterion and risk R17.
- **Lifecycle model separates:** hardware; civil/electrical (trenching-distance-driven); network/software fees; electricity + any demand charges; maintenance; ~10-yr electronics replacement; inspection; decommissioning; contingency (low/base/high with escalation and material volatility). Civil contingency 20–30% at screening maturity, **labeled class-of-estimate, not engineering**.
- **The $429,500 across 12 sites sits within published benchmark ranges but remains a screening value** - it is not a budget, a request, or an estimate of record.

## 5. Economic measure: NPC and avoided cost - ROI explicitly rejected for M2

- **Selected (MCDA winner, 85.8): Net Present Cost comparison** of "install broad capacity now" vs. "starter ports + expandable pathways + trigger-based expansion," with **avoided cost (84.6, effectively tied) as the headline sub-result**: the staged plan avoids unnecessary capex/opex at each horizon - exactly the cost the client actually paid at Neabsco.
- **ROI is explicitly rejected for Milestone 2 (MCDA 52.8):** free/low-fee public charging has no honest revenue line; forcing an ROI would fabricate revenue and breach the project's claims discipline. The Phase 3 rubric's ROI expectation can be met later via avoided-cost framing plus any fee policy the client adopts - flagged now as a client decision (Report 09 §4).
